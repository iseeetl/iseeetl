Tsukuba Institute of Technology (hereinafter referred to as "the University") has established this Privacy Policy (hereinafter, "this Policy") regarding the handling of users' personal information in the services (hereinafter, "the Service") provided on this website.

Article 1 (Personal Information)

"Personal Information" refers to "personal information" as defined by the Personal Information Protection Law, which is information related to a living individual. This information includes name, date of birth, address, phone number, and other descriptions by which a specific individual can be identified, as well as data on appearance, fingerprints, voice prints, and health insurance card numbers or other information from which a specific individual can be identified (personal identification information).

Article 2 (Information We Handle)

The University handles the following information according to the login method, features used, and content entered by the user.

Account, authentication, and profile information

User name, an email address when provided or available, language, profile image, role, display and notification settings, and identifiers needed to link a Google or LINE login. Passwords are not stored as plain text; only password hashes used for verification are stored.

User content

Floor and room names and descriptions, tags, QuickText, posts, replies, supplements, reactions, translations, and uploaded images, video, audio, subtitles, captions, and related information. Guest identifiers and display names are also handled when a guest contributes content.

Usage, device, and security information

Login and session state, memberships and permissions, invitations and access restrictions, operation times, screen and notification settings, cookies and browser storage, and ordinary connection information such as IP address, browser, and device information. Usage analytics is also handled where Google Analytics is enabled.

Inquiry information

Contact details and the contents of an inquiry provided when a user contacts the University.

Date of birth, postal address, and phone number are not required for registration. However, information entered in names, descriptions, posts, or uploaded files is stored and processed as user content. Do not enter personal or confidential information that is not needed for use of the Service.

Article 3 (Purpose of Collection and Use of Personal Information)

The University handles the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To register and authenticate accounts, provide login, and support password reset
  • To provide floors, rooms, timelines, protected media, and other Service functions according to permissions
  • To store and display user content to the users allowed by the applicable settings
  • To provide translation, AI analysis, transcription, and Web Push where those functions are enabled
  • To understand usage and improve the site service
  • To prevent misuse, apply the terms of use, investigate failures, and operate the Service securely
  • To send account-related messages, maintenance information, and important notices
  • To answer inquiries, including identity verification where necessary
  • For purposes reasonably related to those above

Article 4 (Change of Purpose of Use)

The University may change the purpose of using personal information only if it is reasonably considered to be related to the original purpose. In case of a change in purpose, the University will notify the user by a method specified by the University or will announce it on this website.

Article 5 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

The University will not provide personal information to third parties without the prior consent of the user, except in the following cases. However, this does not apply where permitted under the Personal Information Protection Law or other applicable laws.

  • When it is necessary to protect the life, body, or property of an individual and obtaining the person's consent is difficult
  • When it is especially necessary for the improvement of public health or for promoting the healthy upbringing of children and obtaining the person's consent is difficult
  • When there is a need to cooperate with a national or local governmental agency, or an entity entrusted by them, in executing tasks prescribed by laws, and obtaining the person's consent would likely hinder the execution of such tasks
  • When the following matters have been announced or made public in advance and the University has reported them to the Personal Information Protection Commission:
    • The purpose of use includes providing information to third parties
    • The data items provided to the third party
    • The means or method of provision to the third party
    • That the provision of personal information to a third party will be stopped upon request by the person
    • The method of accepting the person's request
  • Regardless of the stipulations in the preceding paragraph, in the following cases, the recipient of the information is not deemed to be a third party:
    • When the University entrusts the handling of personal information, in whole or in part, within the scope necessary to achieve the purpose of use
    • When personal information is provided due to a succession of business resulting from a merger or other circumstances
    • When personal information is used jointly with specific entities and that fact, the items of personal information to be jointly used, the scope of joint users, the purpose for which the users will use the information, and the name or designation of the party responsible for managing that personal information are notified to the person in advance or are placed in a condition easily accessible by the person

Article 6 (Disclosure of Personal Information)

The University will promptly disclose personal information to the individual upon request. However, in cases where disclosing the information meets any of the following criteria, the information may not be fully or partially disclosed. If a decision is made not to disclose, the individual will be promptly notified.

  • When there's a risk of harming the life, body, property, or other rights and interests of the individual or a third party
  • When there's a risk of significantly hindering the proper execution of the University's operations
  • When disclosure would violate other laws

Regardless of the provisions in the preceding paragraph, information other than personal information, such as historical and characteristic information, will not be disclosed as a principle.

Article 7 (Correction and Deletion of Personal Information)

Users, if finding that their personal information held by the University is incorrect, can request the University for the correction, addition, or deletion (hereinafter referred to as "correction, etc.") of their personal information based on the procedures defined by the University.

Upon receiving such a request from a user and determining the need to address the request, the University will promptly make the necessary corrections, etc. to the personal information.

The University will notify the user promptly after having made the corrections, etc. based on the preceding provisions, or after deciding not to make the corrections, etc.

Article 8 (Suspension of Use of Personal Information, etc.)

Upon request from the individual, if the University finds that personal information is being handled beyond the scope of the purpose of use, or has been acquired by fraudulent means, the University will promptly conduct the necessary investigations to suspend or delete (hereinafter referred to as "suspension, etc.") the use of that personal information.

Based on the results of the investigation mentioned in the preceding paragraph and when it's deemed necessary to comply with the request, the University will promptly conduct the suspension, etc. of the personal information.

The University will promptly notify the user after having conducted the suspension, etc. based on the preceding provisions, or after deciding not to do the suspension, etc.

Regardless of the first two paragraphs, in cases where suspending the use of information would incur significant costs or other difficulties, and if alternative measures can be taken to protect the rights and interests of the user, those alternative measures will be adopted.

Article 9 (Changes to the Privacy Policy)

The contents of this policy can be changed without notifying the users unless otherwise stipulated by laws or in this policy.

Unless otherwise defined by the University, the changed privacy policy will take effect from the time it's posted on this website.

Cookies and external transmissions

The Service uses cookies and browser storage and, depending on enabled features or user actions, may use external services for login, fonts, Web Push, translation, AI processing, mail delivery, usage analytics, and sharing. See the Cookie Policy for recipients, information categories, purposes, transmission conditions, retention, and user controls.
