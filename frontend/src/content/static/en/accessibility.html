Our site aims to provide an easily accessible platform where everyone, including the elderly and people with disabilities, can share information and communicate.

Target Compliance Level

We strive to comply with Level AA of "JIS X 8341-3:2016" and "WCAG 2.0".

Scope

The ISeeeTimeLine website

Exceptions

  • The SNS button of X (formerly Twitter)

Past Efforts

  • Functionality for adjusting text size
  • Ability for users to add alternative content
  • AI analysis and addition of information for image, audio, and video content
  • Additional information added by other users
  • Readout functionality for the latest posts
  • Function to play sound when receiving a post with a specified tag
  • Function to stop animations

About the Accessibility of User-Posted Content

On our site, users can freely post content (images, videos, audio) and can optionally provide the following:

  • Alternative text for images
  • Subtitles for videos (subtitle file)
  • Description for audio (alternative content)

However, as we cannot manage or control the content posted by users, there is a possibility that accessibility is not ensured. Additionally, our site introduces a system that uses AI for automatic analysis to generate alternative texts and automatically adds them to the posts to improve the accessibility of posted content.
