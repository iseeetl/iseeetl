This service uses cookies and browser storage to provide the service and save settings, and sends information to third-party services depending on available features and user actions.

1. Cookies and browser storage

Essential cookies

These cookies maintain authentication, display media according to access rights, and process LINE login securely. They are kept only as long as needed for their purpose; authentication cookies are kept for up to 30 days. Blocking or deleting them may prevent login, guest access, or media display from working correctly.

Browser storage

Browser storage saves login state, language, the current location, display settings, and information used to recover from loading failures. Deleting or blocking it may sign you out, reset settings, or disable automatic recovery.

2. Usage analytics with Google Analytics 4

Only in environments where analytics is enabled, we use Google LLC's Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand usage and improve the site service. Normal features continue to work if data cannot be sent to GA4.

Data that may be sent

We may send pseudonymous user identifiers, page and feature usage events, in-service identifiers and names for Floors, Rooms, Tags, and QuickText, and standard information such as browser, device, language, and approximate region to GA4.

Data we do not send

We do not send raw user or guest IDs, email addresses, user names, post, translation, or transcription text, media or file names, authentication information, or search terms as analytics fields defined by this service.

Retention and deletion

GA4 event and user-level data is retained for 14 months, and GA4 cookies may remain for up to 2 years. To request deletion of data associated with you or information sent in error, please contact us.

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Other third-party services

Display and login

Google Fonts receives ordinary connection information when the screen is displayed. Google Identity Services communicates on screens where Google login is available and processes authentication information when you select Google login. LINE receives information needed for authentication only when you select LINE login.

Enabled features

OneSignal, Google Cloud Translation, OpenAI, and the configured mail delivery service receive information needed for notifications, translation, AI analysis or transcription, and mail delivery only when the relevant feature is enabled and used.

User sharing

X or a destination selected through iOS receives the selected sharing content only when you choose to share it. After transmission, the destination handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Your controls and inquiries

You can delete or block cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. You can stop Web Push in your profile and browser notification settings. Optional login and sharing actions do not send authentication information or sharing content unless you select them.

For questions or data deletion requests, please contact us.
